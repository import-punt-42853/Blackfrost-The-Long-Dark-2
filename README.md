## 🕹️ About

BLACKFROST: The Long Dark 2 is a first-person survival sandbox set in the Quiet Apocalypse. One year after the Aurora rendered technology inert, survivors face new dangers in the northern industrial city of Harmont and the wilderness beyond it. Explore, gather resources, manage risk, and work with other survivors or fellow players to endure a changing world. Recent development work has focused on smoother performance, faster loading, and improved memory usage.

A thoughtful survival experience for players who enjoy exploration, resource management, atmospheric worlds, and meaningful choices.

## ✨ Features

- 🧭 **Survival Sandbox** — Scavenge, craft, travel, and make careful decisions across a dynamic environment.
- 🤝 **Shared Survival** — Face the wilderness alone or cooperate with other players.
- 🏙️ **Harmont** — Explore a northern industrial city surrounded by vast, rugged wilderness.
- 👥 **Survivor Communities** — Encounter groups of survivors adapting to life after the Aurora.
- ⚠️ **New Hazards** — Manage environmental threats and dangers unique to Harmont and its surroundings.
- 🏔️ **Quiet Apocalypse** — Survive in a world reshaped by the Aurora and the mysterious black frost.

**Q: How often are updates published?**

**A:** Yes. Contributions in the form of bug reports, feedback, and localization help are welcome. Open an issue or pull request to get started.

**A:** The game is built on a modern engine targeting DirectX 12 on Windows. Technical details are covered in the developer notes and changelog.

**Q: Is there a roadmap?**
**A:** Yes. Development priorities are listed in the project's issue tracker and milestones. Community feedback helps shape what comes next.

**Q: What should I do if the game crashes?**
